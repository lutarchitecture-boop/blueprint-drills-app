Privacy Policy - Blueprint Drills

Last updated: October 2026

Blueprint Drills is a practice-quiz app for architecture professional exam preparation. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Data collection

Blueprint Drills does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function.

What's stored on your device

Your quiz progress (questions answered correctly or incorrectly, grouped by topic) is saved locally on your own device only, using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and is not accessible to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes this data.

Third-party services

This app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other device permissions beyond what the platform requires to run the app itself.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for adult professionals preparing for an architecture licensing exam and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the app's store page.
